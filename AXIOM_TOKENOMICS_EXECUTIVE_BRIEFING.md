## Staking Economics & APR Mechanics

### Dynamic APR Model

AXIOM uses a sophisticated **Energy-Weighted APR** system that rewards both stake size and contribution quality. This prevents whales from dominating rewards while incentivizing active participation.

#### Base APR Calculation

```
Base APR = (Annual Reward Pool / Total Staked) × 100

Year 1 Example:
- Reward Pool: 300,000,000 AXM (from emission schedule)
- Total Staked: 3,000,000,000 AXM (30% of circulation)
- Base APR: (300M / 3B) × 100 = 10%
```

#### Energy Multipliers

The actual APR each staker receives is modified by their "energy score":

**Energy Score Components:**
1. **Stake Duration** (20% weight)
   - < 30 days: 0.5x multiplier
   - 30-90 days: 1.0x multiplier
   - 90-180 days: 1.5x multiplier
   - 180+ days: 2.0x multiplier

2. **Governance Participation** (15% weight)
   - No votes: 0.8x multiplier
   - 1-5 votes: 1.0x multiplier
   - 6-20 votes: 1.3x multiplier
   - 20+ votes: 1.6x multiplier

3. **Platform Usage** (25% weight)
   - No activity: 0.7x multiplier
   - Fractional RE investor: 1.2x multiplier
   - Active trader (liquidity desk): 1.4x multiplier
   - Syndicate participant: 1.5x multiplier

4. **NFT Ownership** (10% weight)
   - No NFT: 1.0x multiplier
   - Bronze NFT: 1.1x multiplier
   - Silver NFT: 1.25x multiplier
   - Gold NFT: 1.5x multiplier

5. **Contribution Score** (30% weight)
   - Based on KeyGrow rent payments, referrals, ecosystem building
   - Range: 0.5x to 3.0x multiplier

**Maximum Possible APR:**
