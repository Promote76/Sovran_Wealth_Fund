## How to Access

1. Navigate to **Enterprise Portal** (`/enterprise`)
2. Click the **🤝 Syndication** tab
3. Click **📖 Info & Guide** sub-tab for comprehensive documentation
4. Use other sub-tabs for functionality:
   - **💼 Syndicate Manager** - Create/manage syndicates
   - **💧 Waterfall Editor** - Configure distributions
   - **✉️ Investor Invitations** - Send invites
