# KeyGrow Rent-to-Own Program - Video Script

**Duration:** 60 seconds  
**Target Audience:** Renters, first-time homebuyers, young professionals  
**Style:** Emotional, hopeful, empowering

---

## SCRIPT

### Opening Hook (0-8s)
**[Visual: Person checking rent payment app, looking frustrated]**

**VOICEOVER:**  
"Paying rent for ten years? That's over 200 thousand dollars... gone. What if every rent payment brought you closer to owning?"

---

### The Problem (8-18s)
**[Visual: Montage of rent checks, landlord letters, moving boxes]**

**VOICEOVER:**  
"You're stuck in the cycle. Rent goes up. Savings go nowhere. Banks say you don't qualify. Meanwhile, your landlord builds equity with your money."

---

### Introducing KeyGrow (18-35s)
**[Visual: AXIOM KeyGrow dashboard, animated pathway from renter to owner]**

**VOICEOVER:**  
"KeyGrow changes everything. Twenty percent of all AXIOM platform revenue goes directly into a Real Estate Acquisition Fund. Your participation earns allocation points. The longer you're in, the higher your tier. Bronze. Silver. Gold. Platinum."

---

### How It Works (35-48s)
**[Visual: Tier progression animation, property selection interface]**

**VOICEOVER:**  
"Stake tokens, provide liquidity, invest in real estate - every action counts. When you're ready, KeyGrow helps with your down payment. You choose the property. We help you own it."

---

### Call to Action (48-60s)
**[Visual: Happy family in front of new home, KeyGrow logo]**

**VOICEOVER:**  
"Stop paying someone else's mortgage. Start building your own future. Join KeyGrow today and turn your rent into ownership."

**[Text on screen: KeyGrow.AXIOM.finance | Register Free]**

**VOICEOVER:**  
"KeyGrow. Your rent deserves better."

---
