## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Settings:** 
- User's home/office (authentic, not staged)
- Natural lighting preferred
- Quiet environment for clean audio

**Camera:**
- Medium close-up (head and shoulders)
- Steady tripod or gimbal
- 1080p minimum, 4K preferred

**Audio:**
- Lavalier mic or shotgun mic
- Room tone for editing
- Clear, conversational tone

**B-Roll Needed:**
- User using AXIOM platform
- Dashboard screenshots (specific to their story)
- Lifestyle shots (home, work, activities)
- Success indicators (charts, wallet, property images)

**Editing Tips:**
- Keep it natural - minimal cuts
- Use B-roll to cover any stumbles
- Add subtle background music (don't overpower voice)
- Include lower thirds with name/credentials
- End with AXIOM logo and URL
