## ⚠️ Legal & Compliance Considerations

### Securities Regulations

**Risk:** Fractional real estate shares may be considered securities

**Mitigation:**
- Consult securities attorney ($2,000-5,000)
- File Reg D 506(c) exemption (allows accredited investors)
- Consider Reg CF (crowdfunding exemption - up to $5M/year)
- Add investor accreditation verification for properties >$100K
- Provide clear risk disclosures

### Insurance Requirements

**Risk:** Smart contract hacks, property damage, user losses

**Mitigation:**
- Partner with Nexus Mutual or InsurAce (DeFi insurance)
- Require property managers to carry E&O insurance
- Platform maintains $1M general liability
- Smart contract audits by CertiK or Quantstamp ($15K-30K)

### Tax Reporting

**Risk:** Users need 1099 forms for earnings

**Mitigation:**
- Integrate with TaxBit or CoinTracker APIs
- Auto-generate 1099-MISC for earnings >$600
- Provide CSV exports for all transactions
- Partner with crypto-savvy CPAs for referrals

### KYC/AML Compliance

**Risk:** Money laundering, sanctions violations

**Mitigation:**
- Implement Persona or Onfido KYC verification
- Screen against OFAC sanctions lists
- Monitor for suspicious transaction patterns
- File SARs (Suspicious Activity Reports) when required
- Limit to US users initially (expand internationally later)
