## 3. REVENUE SHARING

### 3.1 Platform Fees

Platform charges investors the following fees:
- Property investment: 1.6% (Gold tier) to 2.0% (Free tier)
- Monthly rental distributions: 0% (no fee on rental income)
- Property appreciation gains: 0% (no fee on value increase)

### 3.2 Partner Revenue Share

Partner receives **1.0% - 1.5%** (based on tier) of all platform fees generated from:
- Initial property investments
- Secondary market trades
- Annual management fees (if applicable)

**Example Calculation:**
- Property value: $200,000
- Investors pay 2.0% fee = $4,000 platform revenue
- Partner receives 1.5% = $3,000
- Platform keeps 0.5% = $1,000

### 3.3 Payment Terms

- Revenue share paid monthly
- Minimum payout threshold: $100
- Payment via bank transfer or crypto (Partner's choice)
- Detailed monthly statement provided

## 6. PROPERTY LISTING PROCESS

### 6.1 Submission

1. Partner submits property via dashboard
2. Includes: address, photos, financials, tenant info, appraisal
3. Platform reviews within 3 business days

### 6.2 Approval Criteria

Properties must meet:
- Minimum value: $50,000
- Positive cash flow (rent > mortgage + expenses)
- Good condition (no major repairs needed)
- Clear title
- Landlord consent for fractional sale

### 6.3 Listing Launch

1. Platform creates property page
2. Sets fractional share price
3. Markets to investor community
4. Accepts investments until fully funded
5. Closes property and begins distributions

## 7. REVENUE DISTRIBUTION WATERFALL

For each property:

**Monthly Rent Collected** → 
1. Property expenses paid (maintenance, taxes, insurance)
2. Property manager fee (Partner's standard rate, typically 8-12%)
3. Remaining net income distributed to investors proportionally
4. **No platform fee on rental distributions**

**Property Sale/Exit** →
1. Outstanding debts paid
2. Investors receive proportional share of proceeds
3. Platform fee: 2.0% of sale price
4. Partner receives 1.5% of platform fee
